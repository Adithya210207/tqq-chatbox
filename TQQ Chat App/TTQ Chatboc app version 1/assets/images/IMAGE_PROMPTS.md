# ChatGPT prompts for the 5 sister portraits

How to use:
1. Open **one** new ChatGPT chat and keep all 5 pictures in it. Then ChatGPT can reuse
   the same face and style for every sister.
2. Send **Message 1** (setup + Miku). When you like Miku, send Messages 2-5 one at a time.
3. Download each picture you like. Name them `miku.png`, `ichika.png`, `nino.png`,
   `yotsuba.png`, `itsuki.png`, put them in `TQQ Chat App\assets\images\` and restart the app.

Personal fan art for a private app: keep the pictures private.

---

## Message 1: setup + Miku

```
I'm making 5 matching anime portraits for a private desktop chat app, one for each of
the Nakano quintuplets from The Quintessential Quintuplets. I'll ask for them one at a
time in this chat. They are quintuplets, so all 5 must have the SAME face, art style
and lighting; only the hair, accessories, expression, outfit and colour change. Keep
reusing the style of the first picture.

Rules for EVERY picture:
- Tall portrait image (1024 x 1536).
- High-quality modern anime illustration, clean line art, soft cel shading with gentle
  painterly light, like a TV anime key visual.
- Waist-up portrait of one teenage girl, centred, facing the viewer and looking into
  the camera. Large expressive sky-blue eyes.
- Her face is in the upper third of the image (eyes about 25% down from the top), with
  only a little space above the head.
- Soft rim light in her accent colour. The background is a dreamy soft-focus bokeh in
  her accent colour, fading to near-black at the bottom.
- The bottom 40% stays simple and darker (my app puts text there). Nothing important
  near the left and right edges (my app crops the sides).
- No text, no logo, no watermark, no frame, no other people.

Picture 1: MIKU NAKANO, the third sister. Accent colour: sky blue (#5aa9ff).
Medium-long reddish-brown/pink hair, with long bangs partly covering one eye. Blue
over-ear headphones resting around her neck. Quiet, shy, gentle expression with a faint
blush and a tiny hesitant smile. A blue cardigan over a white blouse. Calm, introverted
mood.
```

## If ChatGPT refuses (copyright)
ChatGPT sometimes won't draw named anime characters. If that happens, send the same
message again with the names and the show title removed. Write "an anime girl" instead
of "MIKU NAKANO, the third sister", and in Message 1 replace the first sentence with:
`I'm making 5 matching anime portraits of five identical sisters (quintuplets).`
The descriptions still give each girl her look.

## Fixing a picture
Reply in the same chat, for example:
- `Same picture, but move her higher so her face is in the top third.`
- `Same picture, but make the background darker at the bottom.`
- `Same face as picture 1, please. She looks too different.`

## Why the prompts ask for this (how the app uses the pictures)
| Where | What the app does with the picture |
|---|---|
| Home panels | Crops a tall, narrow slice from the **centre**, so she must be centred |
| Bottom 40% | Darkened and covered by her name, so keep it simple |
| Chat avatars | Cuts a circle from the **top** of the picture, so the face must be high |
| Chat background | Blurs it heavily, so her colour matters more than detail |
